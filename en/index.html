The story

From acacia to ember.

The journey every EKOOLOGY bag makes — from invaded forest to your grill.

  1. The invader

    Acacia is one of Portugal's most invasive species: it smothers the native forest, dries out the soil and burns like gunpowder.

  2. The clearing

    In Penacova we remove acacia from the forest: every bag takes wildfire fuel away and gives space back to native species.

  3. Solar pyrolysis

    We turn it into what it does best — dense, high-energy wood — through slow pyrolysis powered by solar energy. The result: over 94% fixed carbon.

  4. The ember

    More heat, less smoke and embers that last. And, in partnership with WWF Portugal (ANP), a percentage of sales funds the reforestation of cleared areas.
