Clams Bulhão Pato over embers

Lisbon's classic with a kiss of smoke: clams open in a pan set right on the embers, with garlic, coriander and a squeeze of lemon.

⏱ Prep: 15 min 🔥 Cook: 10 min 👥 Serves: 4 Direct heat Difficulty: Fácil

Ingredients

  • 1 kg fresh clams (soaked 2 h in salted water to purge sand)
  • 6 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 bunch coriander
  • 100 ml olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Heat a wide pan directly over hot embers.
  2. Add the olive oil and garlic; let it turn golden without burning.
  3. Add the clams and cover for 3–4 minutes, shaking the pan.
  4. When they have all opened, squeeze over the lemon and shower with chopped coriander.
  5. Discard any that stayed shut and serve in the pan.
🌡 Fire & temperature
Hot embers · lid off only at the end — the steam is what opens the shells.
