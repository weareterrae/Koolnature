Lamb chops with rosemary

Fast, fragrant and pink: lamb fat melts over medium embers and rosemary does the rest. Eaten by hand, no apologies.

⏱ Prep: 10 min 🔥 Cook: 10 min 👥 Serves: 4 Direct heat Difficulty: Fácil

Ingredients

  • 12 lamb chops
  • 2 sprigs rosemary, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Marinate the chops 30 minutes in oil, garlic, rosemary and pepper.
  2. Prepare medium embers — too fierce burns the fat before it melts.
  3. Grill 3–4 minutes per side, salting as you turn.
  4. Pull at 52 °C for 54–57 °C on the plate (medium-rare).
  5. Rest 5 minutes and serve stacked, fresh rosemary on top.
🌡 Fire & temperature
54–57 °C medium-rare · lamb fat wants medium embers — it should melt, not flare.
