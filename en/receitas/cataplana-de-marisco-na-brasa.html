Seafood cataplana over embers

The whole sea in one pot: clams, prawns and firm fish opening in white-wine steam, the copper cataplana resting on the embers. Opened at the table, applause guaranteed.

⏱ Prep: 20 min 🔥 Cook: 25 min 👥 Serves: 4 Indirect heat Difficulty: Média

Ingredients

  • 500 g clams
  • 400 g prawns
  • 400 g firm fish (monkfish or meagre) in cubes
  • 1 onion, 1 pepper and 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, 150 ml white wine
  • Olive oil, coriander, piri-piri and salt

Method

  1. Layer the cataplana: onion, pepper, tomato, garlic, then fish and shellfish.
  2. Drizzle with olive oil and white wine; season with salt and piri-piri.
  3. Close it and set over medium-gentle embers.
  4. Leave 20–25 minutes without opening — the steam does all the work.
  5. Open at the table, shower with coriander, and bring bread for the broth.
🌡 Fire & temperature
Medium-gentle embers and patience: a cataplana never boils in fury, it whispers.
