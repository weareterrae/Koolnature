Octopus lagareiro-style… on the grill

First simmered until tender, then over hot embers until the tips crackle. With punched potatoes and a river of garlic olive oil — lagareiro done properly.

⏱ Prep: 20 min 🔥 Cook: 40 min 👥 Serves: 4 Direct heat Difficulty: Média

Ingredients

  • 1 octopus (±2 kg), simmered until tender
  • 800 g small potatoes, skin on
  • 6 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 200 ml olive oil
  • Coarse salt and parsley

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water; give each a gentle punch to split it open.
  2. Pat the cooked tentacles very dry — wet octopus won't caramelise.
  3. Grill over hot embers 3–4 minutes per side, until the tips crackle.
  4. Warm the olive oil with the garlic without letting it fry.
  5. Pile octopus and potatoes on a platter and drown everything in the hot oil.
