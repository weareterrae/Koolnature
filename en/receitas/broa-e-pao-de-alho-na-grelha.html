Grilled corn bread and garlic bread

The starter that opens any barbecue: Portuguese corn bread and country loaf toasted over embers, with garlic butter melting into the grill marks.

⏱ Prep: 10 min 🔥 Cook: 8 min 👥 Serves: 4 Direct heat Difficulty: Fácil

Ingredients

  • 1/2 Portuguese corn bread (broa), thickly sliced
  • 1/2 country loaf, sliced
  • 100 g softened butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • Chopped parsley
  • Flaky sea salt

Method

  1. Mix the butter with the garlic, parsley and a pinch of salt.
  2. Butter the slices generously on both sides.
  3. Grill over medium-hot embers, 2–3 minutes per side, until marked and toasted.
  4. Serve hot, stacked, to tear by hand.
🌡 Fire & temperature
Medium-hot embers · 2–3 min per side, never unattended — bread goes from toasted to burnt in seconds.
