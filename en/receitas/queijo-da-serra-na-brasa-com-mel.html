Ember-baked Serra cheese with honey

A whole buttery cheese warmed on the gentle side of the grill until it wobbles, finished with honey and rosemary. A party starter in ten minutes.

⏱ Prep: 5 min 🔥 Cook: 10 min 👥 Serves: 4 Indirect heat Difficulty: Fácil

Ingredients

  • 1 small Serra da Estrela cheese (or any soft buttery cheese)
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 sprig rosemary
  • Chopped walnuts (optional)
  • Toast or corn bread to serve

Method

  1. Set up two heat zones; the cheese goes over the side WITHOUT embers.
  2. Cut a thin lid off the top of the rind.
  3. Warm for 8–10 minutes with the lid closed (or a bowl inverted over it), until the centre wobbles.
  4. Drizzle with honey, add rosemary and walnuts, and serve with spoons at the ready.
🌡 Fire & temperature
Gentle indirect heat · the cheese never sits over the embers — it should melt, not cook.
