From the garden

Vegetable board with ember salt

The garden proving it can take the fire: asparagus, corn, peppers, courgette and portobello, each on its own clock, all kissed by sweet acacia smoke.

⏱ Prep: 15 min 🔥 Cook: 20 min 👥 Serves: 4 Direct heat Difficulty: Fácil

Ingredients

  • 1 bunch asparagus
  • 2 pre-boiled corn cobs
  • 2 peppers
  • 1 courgette in 1 cm slices
  • 4 portobello mushrooms
  • Olive oil, flaky salt, lemon

Method

  1. Brush everything with olive oil; salt only at the end.
  2. Whole peppers first: 15–20 minutes over hot embers, turning, until the skin blisters.
  3. Corn: 12–15 minutes over medium embers, rotating.
  4. Courgette and portobello: 6–10 minutes over medium embers.
  5. Asparagus last: 4–6 minutes, always laid across the bars.
  6. Pile onto the board with salt flakes, lemon zest and your best olive oil.
