Piri-piri chicken, Guia style

Spatchcocked chicken, golden skin and the spicy sauce that made the Algarve famous. Patience over indirect heat, courage over direct.

⏱ Prep: 15 min 🔥 Cook: 45 min 👥 Serves: 4 Direct + indirect Difficulty: Média

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, spatchcocked
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp red-pepper paste
  • Piri-piri to taste
  • 50 ml olive oil + 30 g butter
  • Juice of 1 lemon, salt

Method

  1. Season the chicken the day before with garlic, pepper paste, salt and lemon.
  2. Set up two zones: start the bird on the indirect side, skin up.
  3. Cover and cook 35–45 minutes, turning halfway.
  4. Check: 74 °C in the breast, 80 °C in the thigh (probe away from bone).
  5. Finish 3–4 minutes over direct embers, skin down, to crisp.
  6. Brush with the oil, butter and piri-piri sauce when serving.
🌡 Fire & temperature
74 °C breast / 80 °C thigh — poultry has rules, not doneness levels. Pull at 72 °C and rest 8 minutes.
