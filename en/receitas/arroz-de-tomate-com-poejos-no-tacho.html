Tomato rice with pennyroyal in a clay pot

Our flagship dish: soupy tomato rice cooked in a clay pot ringed by embers — with pennyroyal added only at the end, as the wise ones insist.

⏱ Prep: 15 min 🔥 Cook: 30 min 👥 Serves: 4 Indirect heat Difficulty: Média

Ingredients

  • 300 g carolino (or arborio) rice
  • 600 g ripe tomatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 1 onion and 3 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1.2 L hot water
  • 1 bunch fresh pennyroyal (or mint)
  • Olive oil, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Arrange the embers in a ring and set the clay pot in the centre.
  2. Soften the onion and garlic in olive oil until golden.
  3. Add the tomato and cook down for 10 minutes, stirring.
  4. Pour in the hot water; when it boils, add the rice.
  5. Cook 15–18 minutes, stirring now and then — it should stay soupy.
  6. Off the heat, add the pennyroyal, cover for 2 minutes and serve loose.
🌡 Fire & temperature
Embers in a ring (never underneath!) — clay wants gentle, enveloping heat, never thermal shock.
