Tiger prawns with garlic and piri-piri

Big prawns, crackly shells, juicy centres — and that garlic, butter and piri-piri sauce that demands more bread.

⏱ Prep: 15 min 🔥 Cook: 6 min 👥 Serves: 4 Direct heat Difficulty: Fácil

Ingredients

  • 12 large tiger prawns, shell on
  • 4 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 80 g butter
  • 1 tsp piri-piri (or to taste)
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Chopped coriander and salt

Method

  1. Slit the prawns down the back and remove the vein, keeping the shell.
  2. Melt the butter with the garlic and piri-piri; brush the prawns.
  3. Grill over hot embers 2–3 minutes per side, basting with the sauce.
  4. They're ready when opaque and curled into a C.
  5. Finish with lemon and coriander; serve piping hot.
🌡 Fire & temperature
Hot embers · 49–52 °C inside; curled in a C = done, closed in an O = overdone.
