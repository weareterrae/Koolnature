Fondant banana with dark chocolate

The campfire dessert, grown-up edition: banana roasted in its own skin, stuffed with dark chocolate that melts inside. Spoon mandatory.

⏱ Prep: 5 min 🔥 Cook: 10 min 👥 Serves: 4 Indirect heat Difficulty: Fácil

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe bananas, skin on
  • 80 g dark chocolate squares
  • 1 pinch flaky salt
  • Cinnamon (optional)
  • Crushed biscuit to sprinkle (optional)

Method

  1. Slit each banana lengthwise without separating the halves — skin stays on.
  2. Tuck the chocolate squares into the slit.
  3. No wrapping needed: onto the indirect zone, 8–10 minutes.
  4. Ready when the skin darkens and the chocolate peeks out, melted.
  5. Pinch of salt on top and dive in with a spoon.
🌡 Fire & temperature
Indirect heat · the skin is the baking tin: dark outside, fondant inside.
