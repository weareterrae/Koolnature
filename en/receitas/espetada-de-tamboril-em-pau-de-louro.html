Monkfish skewers on bay branches

Madeira-style: monkfish cubes threaded on bay-laurel branches that perfume the fish from within while the embers work the outside.

⏱ Prep: 20 min 🔥 Cook: 10 min 👥 Serves: 4 Direct heat Difficulty: Média

Ingredients

  • 800 g monkfish loin in large cubes
  • 8 sturdy bay branches (or skewers with bay leaves between cubes)
  • 150 g bacon squares (optional)
  • Olive oil, crushed garlic, salt and pepper
  • 1 lemon

Method

  1. Season the monkfish with oil, garlic, salt and pepper; rest 20 minutes.
  2. Thread the cubes onto the bay branches, alternating with bacon.
  3. Grill over medium-hot embers, turning every 2–3 minutes.
  4. At 8–10 minutes the fish should be opaque and firm (55 °C at the centre).
  5. Squeeze over lemon and serve on the branch.
🌡 Fire & temperature
Target 55 °C at the centre · pull at 53–54 °C: monkfish keeps cooking off the grill.
